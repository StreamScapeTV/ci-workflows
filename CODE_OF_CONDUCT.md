# Code of Conduct

We want participation in `ci-workflows` to be respectful, technical and welcoming to contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate constructively, particularly when discussing CI failures or design disagreements.
- Provide reproducible, appropriately sanitized evidence and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect others' time, privacy and technical boundaries; accept thoughtful review and correction.
- Welcome questions and feedback without insults or personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, sexualized comments directed at others, intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and deliberate abuse of project infrastructure are not acceptable. Publicly sharing credentials, private CI logs, personal information or confidential vulnerability details is also not acceptable.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. If a discussion violates this policy, use GitHub's built-in **Report content** / abuse reporting controls. For concerns requiring a private maintainer response, request an appropriate private contact method without publishing the sensitive details in an issue. Security vulnerabilities should instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Moderation decisions will consider severity, pattern, safety and the context of the interaction. No private reporting address or guaranteed moderation response time is asserted here until one is established.
